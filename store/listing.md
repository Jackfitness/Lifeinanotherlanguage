## Description

Move into a cozy village where your plush neighbors only speak Japanese.

Life in Another Language is a gentle life-sim that teaches Japanese by living it. Chat with friendly villagers, reply in Japanese, run errands, go fishing, and learn the words you need along the way.

LEARN TO READ AND WRITE — FREE
Start in Inkbrush Village, free and unlimited. Trace hiragana, katakana and your first kanji by hand, then pass the Script Exam.

REAL CONVERSATIONS
Every villager speaks natural Japanese with voiced lines. Show kana only, kanji with furigana, or plain kanji, and tap any line to peek at its meaning.

SIX TOWNS, FROM FIRST KANA TO ADVANCED
Travel by bus from beginner (N5) to advanced (N1) levels. Each town has its own story, new neighbors, a dojo full of bite-size lessons, and an exam to pass before the bell rings.

LESSONS THAT STICK
Grammar, vocabulary, listening and reading in short dojo lessons. Words you meet go into your notebook, and spaced review brings them back right before you'd forget them.

LOOK AROUND
Walk up to a fountain, a lamp or a bridge and see what it's called in Japanese.

PLAY YOUR WAY
English or Spanish menus. Adjustable voice speed. Short sessions that fit any day.

FREE TO START
Inkbrush Village is free with no limits. The first chapter of Clover Meadow is free too, with 15 minutes of village play a day.

VILLAGE PASS
Unlock every town and play as long as you like:
• Lifetime: one-time purchase
• Monthly: 7-day free trial, then billed monthly

Monthly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple ID is charged at confirmation (after the free trial, if any). Manage or cancel any time in your Apple ID Settings.
