Life in Another Language

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Life in Another Language is a game for learning Japanese. We built it to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your name for your plush, your progress, words you've learned and your settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Village Pass is sold through the App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google handle payment; we never see your card or account details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own the Pass. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your purchase history for this app, and uses them only to provide that service.

Fonts

Some lettering is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when the fonts download, as with any website. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
